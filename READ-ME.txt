After the Answer — V7.5
Complete story development build

PLAY
1. Extract the entire archive to a stable folder. Do not run it inside a ZIP.
2. Open start.html in a recent Chrome, Edge, or Firefox.
3. If local-file access is restricted, macOS users may run
   Start-local-server.command. It uses an existing Python 3 installation and
   installs nothing. If double-clicking is restricted, run:

   bash Start-local-server.command

   On other platforms with Python 3, open a terminal in this folder and run:

   python3 -m http.server 56607 --bind 127.0.0.1 --directory game

   Then visit:

   http://127.0.0.1:56607/v75/start.html?lang=en

SCENE REHEARSAL
The title menu offers eleven story interactions to rehearse, including accident
evacuation, archive provenance, the local council, and later scenes. Rehearsals
do not change story saves; reloading the page resets the rehearsal.

MINIGAMES
The title screen links to Little Orbit and Workshop. Their saves remain separate
from the story. Little Orbit uses the arrow keys to move, Space to drop, 1/2 to
switch claws, and P to pause.

CONTROLS
Click scene markers or sidebar entries and switch between Vika and Noyi. Use the
hint control when needed. Tab selects, Enter activates, and Escape closes dialogs.
Motion and sound are optional.

SAVES
The opening, Jaipur, London, and the continuation use four separate saves.
Starting or continuing does not reset them. Journey Backup and Restore on the
title screen transfers all four sections after a reviewed confirmation.

An empty section in a backup clears its matching destination save. Unsubmitted
puzzle drafts, audio preferences, and minigames are not included. Web URLs,
local-file paths, browser profiles, and different browsers may use separate
storage areas. Export a journey backup before changing any of them.

This release contains no player progress. The game does not upload saves.

LANGUAGES
English is the default public presentation. Simplified Chinese, Arabic, Hindi,
and Japanese are also available in the game. Arabic uses right-to-left layout.
Translations other than the Chinese controlling text remain working drafts and
have not received full native-speaker review.

BUILD STATUS
This is a complete playable story build, not a signed native application or a
release-certified commercial product. Art, sound, character animation,
translations, and interactions remain in development and were produced with AI
assistance. SHA256SUMS.txt lists every shipped file and its SHA-256 checksum.
